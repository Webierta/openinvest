OpenInvest is an open-source application designed for investors seeking detailed, analytical, and 100% private tracking of their investment fund portfolios.

Key Features:
- Real-time fund search and quotes using ISIN codes (CNMV, Morningstar, and Yahoo Finance).
- Professional-grade financial performance metrics: TAE, TWR, and MWR/TIR.
- Fund comparator: evaluate two funds side-by-side with normalized charts and performance comparison.
- Annual PDF reports and CSV operations export.
- Complete portfolio backups with smart merge.
- Cost and fee audit (TER impact assessment).
- Interactive evolution charts and monthly return heatmaps.
- Benchmark comparison against major global indices (S&P 500, MSCI World, etc.).
- Advanced risk analysis: Max Drawdown and recovery time.
- Multicurrency support with real-time conversion.
- Total privacy: ad-free, tracker-free, with 100% local storage and biometric/password security.
